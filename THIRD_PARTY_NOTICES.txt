## BeadColors reference RGB data

Source: https://github.com/maxcleme/beadcolors
Data: https://github.com/maxcleme/beadcolors/blob/master/raw/perler.csv
License: https://github.com/maxcleme/beadcolors/blob/master/LICENSE

The Perler Standard screen reference RGB values used in `src/data/perler.json` and `src/data/perler-6000.json` are derived from BeadColors; relevant rows credit contributor Perlervault. HEX values are conversions of those RGB values. These are community reference values, not official Perler manufacturing color specifications. Clear and Neon display placeholders are authored approximations and excluded from automatic matching.

The original license follows in full.

Additional catalogs (2026-10-15): `src/data/hama-midi.json` uses BeadColors `raw/hama.csv` RGB/name rows under this same MIT license. `src/data/artkal-s.json` uses names from `raw/artkal_s.csv` under MIT, with numerical RGB facts transcribed from Artkal's published 2024 S Midi RGB chart. Raw CSV snapshots and the Artkal PDF are retained in data-sources/; download links and catalog scope remain in the JSON metadata for maintenance. These metadata are not shown in the user interface.
